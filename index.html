Welcome to Campus Bondhon — Stronger Together on Campus

Campus Bondhon is a dedicated connection hub for university students and nearby residential landlords. Pass on your textbooks and notes, donate surplus clothes with 100% privacy protection, or discover verified vacant rooms and flats within walking distance of campus.

📚 Browse Study Resources 👕 Anonymous Clothing Box 🏢 Find Student Housing

How Campus Bondhon Works

Connecting seniors, juniors, and local landlords in three simple steps.

1

Explore or Post a Listing

Share surplus semester books, donate clean clothes anonymously, or list vacant student rooms using our mobile-friendly forms.

2

Filter by Your Needs

Filter books by department, clothing by size, or student flats by monthly rent and availability in seconds.

3

Connect Directly on WhatsApp

Click the one-tap WhatsApp or Call button to coordinate a quick campus handoff—with full dignity and zero middlemen.
